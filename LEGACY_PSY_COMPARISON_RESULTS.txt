INTERPRETATION NOTES
--------------------
- 3-between x 3-within: One 18-contrast all-factorial family; selector-by-selector cell means are dropped before correction.
- between-subjects 2 x 2 SMR: Psy uses deterministic quadrature; PsyR uses the vignette's seeded 100,000-draw simulation, so its critical constant is approximate.
- J x K pooled post-hoc: Expected critical-constant difference: original Psy uses all 12 cell groups (11 df); the PsyR workflow explicitly supplies nu1 = 8.
- planned mixed simple effects: Psy's user-supplied-constant route is used for the selected pooled family; its standard interface would generate unwanted cross-products.
